# **App Name**: Avocado Export Hub

## Core Features:

- Hero Section with Key Figures: Visually appealing hero section with background image and key statistics on Moroccan avocado exports.
- Quality Assurance Showcase: Display of quality certifications with a brief description of the company's commitment to quality avocado exports.
- Avocado Varieties Section: Dedicated section to showcase the different varieties of Moroccan avocados exported, each with a description and image.
- Client Testimonials: Rotating testimonials from satisfied clients.
- Trusted Partners Section: Showcase of the partners with their logos to inspire customer trust.
- Blog Section: A feed of the latest blog posts. It links directly to posts containing useful and relevant information.
- Contact Information: Contact details for export inquiries, including address, phone, and social media, shown with a simple map visualization of the location.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Warm yellow (#F4D03F) to reflect the natural and vibrant quality of avocados.
- Background color: Desaturated warm yellow (#F8F7F2) for a clean and natural backdrop.
- Accent color: Complementary olive green (#A2B572) to highlight the product's organic nature.
- Headline font: 'Playfair', a modern serif with an elegant, high-end feel.
- Body font: 'PT Sans', a humanist sans-serif that complements 'Playfair' for a balanced and readable design.
- Simple, clean icons representing key aspects such as export volume, customer satisfaction, and product quality.
- Clear, section-based layout for easy navigation.
- Smooth transitions and parallax scrolling to enhance user engagement.
